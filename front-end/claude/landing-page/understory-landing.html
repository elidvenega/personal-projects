What a walk actually looks like

01

Meet at the trailhead

We start with a short briefing on what's fruiting this week, basic safety, and how to forage without damaging the mycelium below.

02

Walk and identify

We move slowly. Every find gets examined on the spot — cap, gills, spore color, smell — before anything goes in a basket.

03

Take a spore print home

Back at the trailhead, we help you make a spore print of your best find, so you leave with something beyond a photo.
